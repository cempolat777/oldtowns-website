SYSTEMIC ROOT CAUSES FIXED

1. City contexts now use division bounding-box centers instead of southwest
   corners.
2. Generic labels such as "Full Walk" and unsafe title tokens such as "long"
   are removed from verified route evidence.
3. Title-derived place guessing is disabled.
4. Route matches are rejected outside city-aware distance limits.
5. Nearby hotels require a trusted video anchor and are checked against the
   anchor again at render time.
6. Hotel category matching is strict and Latin hotel names are preferred when
   available.
7. Old incompatible location caches are invalidated.
8. Repeated publication dates are removed from generated H1 titles.
9. A transaction-like repair command audits all videos before changing the
   source data.
